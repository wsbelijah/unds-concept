About the Brand

Built Like the Vintage It Studies

UNDS is elevated street heritage — designed and made in Los Angeles, and built the slow way. Heavyweight Japanese fabrics, American-milled terry, Italian leather hardware. Pieces start from real vintage references — a 1940s crewneck, a broken-in pair of selvedge — and get rebuilt with modern construction until they hold their structure and age like the originals.

Everything is premade and limited. Drops land Saturdays at 11AM PST, and when a piece is gone, it's gone — the archive above is the proof.
